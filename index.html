The Meaning of Your Name

NOVA

From the Latin word for 'new.' In the night sky, a nova is a star that suddenly shines so bright it seems to appear out of nowhere. That is exactly how you came into my life.

GRACE

A gift from God that is never earned, only received. That is what you are to me.

A new star. A gift from God. Nova Grace.

A Birthday Letter

Happy birthday, my love.

On January 24, 2025, I sent a message to someone I only knew from TikTok. I did not know I was messaging the person I would end up loving. We started with tech stacks and a streak. Then we became frenny. And then, little by little, I could not hide my feelings anymore.

Thank you for being genuine, consistent, and kind. Thank you for every update, every prayer, and every morning we opened His Word together. Thank you for staying after I told you how I felt.

On your birthday, I pray that God gives you more than you ask for: peace in your heart, strength on your tired days, joy in the small things, and a life that stays full of His love.

And I pray that I get to celebrate many more birthdays with you.

I love you, Nova Grace.

Yours,
John Carlo
